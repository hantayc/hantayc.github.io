I’m a Data Scientist with an M.S. in Information and Data Science from UC Berkeley and 5+ years turning data into decisions across consulting and financial services. I started in pharmaceutical analytics consulting — supporting three enterprise clients, including two Fortune 100 companies and a top-35 global pharmaceutical firm — before moving into digital, CX, and operational analytics at [AIG](https://www.aig.com) and later [Corebridge Financial](https://www.corebridgefinancial.com), following the spin-off of its Individual Retirement and Life businesses.

My work spans **applied NLP and GenAI**, **time-series forecasting**, and **causal experimentation** — taking problems from initial framing through modeling to deployment and executive decision-making. I’ve led initiatives that reversed double-digit CSAT declines, improved workforce planning through predictive forecasting, and generated six-figure cost savings through automation.
